v0.0.1
- First release.

Katan is a Netflix-style front end for Kodi 21 in Hebrew and English:
Israeli live television and on-demand, films and series through TMDB and
Trakt, anime, and automatic Hebrew subtitles with an AI translation
fallback for anything that has none.

Playback of films and series needs a debrid account - TorBox,
Real-Debrid, Premiumize or AllDebrid. Live television, the on-demand
catalogue and anime need nothing at all.

Written for weak hardware: bounded concurrency, capped caches, no skin
fork, and a 460 KB add-on.
